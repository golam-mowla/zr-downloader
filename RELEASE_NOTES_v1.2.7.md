# ZR Downloader v1.2.7 🚀

Latest official release of **ZR Downloader** with native support for Windows and Android.

---

### 🌟 What's New in v1.2.7:
1. **Low Disk Space Mode (Zero-Temp In-Place)**:
   - Saves 50% SSD space on large downloads (e.g. a 20 GB file takes strictly 20 GB instead of 40 GB).
   - Parallel chunks write directly into the final destination file without duplicate temporary segment files or assembly delay. Perfect for 220 GB SSDs.
2. **Accurate YouTube Video Qualities**:
   - Dynamic internal player extraction only displays qualities actually available for the video (e.g. 360p, 240p, 144p).
   - No more fake 1080p options on low-res videos.
3. **Download Video Thumbnails & Subtitles**:
   - High-resolution video thumbnail (JPG) and multi-language subtitle tracks (VTT) can now be downloaded directly from the IDM-style overlay dropdown.
4. **Instagram Video & Audio Support**:
   - Fixed video overlay anchor alignment for Instagram Reels & Feed.
   - Intrinsic resolution detection (1080p, 720p, 480p) and direct MP3 audio extraction with cover image downloads.
5. **Full Rebranding to ZR Downloader**:
   - Completely migrated all code, application packages, and repository descriptions to **ZR Downloader**.
